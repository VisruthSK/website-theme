This site shows the theme with the colours and type of astro-erudite. Apart from the benchmark essay and the note that shows every feature, its posts are public domain stories from Project Gutenberg.

The theme’s tests run against this site. They check the typesetting, the footnotes, the citations, the search page, the feeds and the accessibility of every page, in light and dark mode.
