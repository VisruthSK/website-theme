This site shows what the theme looks like with ordinary content, in the colours and type of astro-erudite. Most of the essays and notes are placeholder text written for testing, and the people and books they mention are invented. The exception is [Measuring the theme against astro-erudite](/essays/measuring-the-theme/), which reports real measurements.

The theme's tests run against this site. They check the typesetting, the footnotes, the citations, the search page, the feeds and the accessibility of every page, in light and dark mode.
