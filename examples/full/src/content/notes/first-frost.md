title: 'First Frost'
date: 2024-10-28
description: 'The garden on the first cold morning of the year.'
tags: ['garden']
---

The first frost arrived overnight, a week earlier than last year. By eight the lawn was white and stiff, and the last of the runner beans hung black on their canes. The leeks and the kale did not seem to mind at all.

Frost is a useful deadline. It ends the arguments about whether to leave the tomatoes another week, and it marks the point where the garden stops being a place of production and becomes a place of planning. The seed catalogues come out tonight.
