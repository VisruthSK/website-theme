title: "Publishing to Standard.site"
date: 2026-11-03
description: "Publish the master RSS feed to the AT Protocol."
tags: ["features"]
---

The theme can publish its master RSS feed to [Standard.site](https://standard.site/). Posts are published under one publication, and edits update the same records. See the [setup instructions](https://github.com/VisruthSK/website-theme#standardsite) for configuration.
