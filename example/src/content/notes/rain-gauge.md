---
title: 'The Rain Gauge'
date: 2025-05-18
description: 'What a plastic tube on a fence post has taught me.'
tags: ['garden', 'nature']
---

A rain gauge costs almost nothing and changes how you see the weather. After a year of readings I know that the wettest month here is not the one everybody complains about, and that a "heavy shower" usually adds up to less than five millimetres.

The habit matters more than the instrument: read it at the same time each morning, write the number down, empty it. The records become interesting only after the first year, when there is something to compare against.
