A few books I return to when I want to think about how things are made:

- A history of printing, mostly for the pictures of old type cases.
- A field guide to rivers, which turned into the [essay on rivers](/essays/rivers/).
- A collection of letters between two gardeners, published by [Example Press](https://example.org/press).

None of them are long, and all of them reward a second reading.
